# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in the FTorch project, and in particular in the
issue tracker, pull requests, and social media activity, is expected to treat
other people with respect and, more generally, to follow the guidelines
articulated in the
[Python Community Code of Conduct](https://www.python.org/psf/codeofconduct/).


## Our Standards

The FTorch community aims to be a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone,
regardless of background or identity.
We expect all participants to:

- Be friendly and patient.
- Be welcoming to newcomers and those with different levels of experience.
- Be considerate of others' viewpoints and experiences.
- Be respectful in all interactions.
- Be careful in the words that we choose, remembering that we are a worldwide
  community.
- Gracefully accept constructive criticism.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.


## Unacceptable Behaviour

Behaviour that is not acceptable includes, but is not limited to:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Offensive comments related to gender, sexual orientation, race, religion,
  disability, or other personal characteristics.
- Inappropriate use of sexual imagery or language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or insulting/derogatory comments.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of talks, discussions, or other community activities.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.


## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces managed by the FTorch project,
including but not limited to:

- The GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions).
- Social media activity related to the project.
- Conferences, workshops, and other events where the project is represented.
- Any other forums or communication channels used by the community.


## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported by contacting the [ICCS team](https://github.com/Cambridge-ICCS).
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the
project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the
reporter of an incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct may face
temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the project leadership.
